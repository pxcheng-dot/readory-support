1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you register on the Services, add family members, keep a reading journal, use optional features, or contact us.

Personal Information Provided by You. Depending on how you use ReadiBean, this may include:

  • Account holder name and email address
  • Sign-in and authentication data (email and password, or Sign in with Apple or Google)
  • Child profiles you create (first name, optional nickname, optional birth date or age, optional gender, optional photo or avatar, optional color tag)
  • Reading journal data (titles, authors, covers, dates, notes, gifts, minutes, rereads, ratings)
  • Optional holiday or heritage preferences used only to tailor calendar-aware book suggestions
  • Family sharing details (invited email addresses and household roles)
  • Support messages you send us, which may include device and app diagnostics you attach
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information to keep the app working

Sensitive Information. When necessary, with your consent or as otherwise permitted by applicable law, we process:

  • Children’s personal information that a parent or caregiver enters into a profile or journal
  • Optional cultural or heritage preferences if you choose them for holiday suggestions

We do not use sensitive information to advertise to children or to infer unrelated characteristics about a child.

Payment Data. If you subscribe or buy a pack, payment is handled and stored by Apple. We receive subscription and purchase status, not your full card number. See Apple’s privacy notice: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Social Login Data. You may register with Apple or Google. We receive the profile information described in Section 7.

Application Data. If you grant permission, we may collect:

  • Geolocation Information. Optional, when the app is in use. A one-time check can show nearby public libraries, local holiday suggestions, and the right bookstores for your country. We do not continuously track location and we do not use location to track children. You can change this in your device settings.
  • Mobile Device Access. Camera (to snap a book cover or barcode); photos you choose (covers or a child’s avatar). You can change these permissions in your device settings.
  • Push Notifications. Optional local bedtime reminders. Turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

All personal information you provide must be true, complete, and accurate. Please update it when it changes.

Information from public catalogs

To fill in a book’s title, author, and cover, we may query public catalogs using an ISBN or title. Those lookups are not tied to a child’s name.

Google API

Our use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1A. HOW DO WE TREAT CHILDREN’S INFORMATION?

In Short: ReadiBean is for parents and caregivers. We do not require a child to create an account.

The signed-in adult is the account holder. Child profiles exist so the journal can be age-right. We collect a child’s information only when a parent or caregiver enters it.

We do not sell children’s personal information. We do not show advertising to children. We do not use children’s reading data for targeted advertising.

A parent or caregiver can review, correct, or delete a child’s profile and journal in the app (including Settings → Privacy & Data) or by emailing support@ireadit.app. Deleting the app from a device does not by itself delete a signed-in cloud account.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We may also process your information for other purposes only with your prior explicit consent.

We process personal information:

  • To create and manage accounts and keep the family library in working order, including multi-device sync when you sign in.
  • To provide the journal: timeline, Discover, routines, reminders, milestones, and optional reading games (Living Library / Goodnight Nest).
  • To personalize book suggestions from books you have already logged.
  • To run optional Pro librarian features (for example a short “why this book” line) through our own backend. API keys never live in the app.
  • To restore purchases and honor subscriptions billed by Apple.
  • To respond to support requests and request feedback.
  • To protect our Services, including security and fraud prevention.
  • To understand usage at a high level so we can fix problems and improve the product.
  • To comply with law and to protect an individual’s vital interests where required.

In Short: We share information with service providers who operate ReadiBean for us, and in the limited situations below. We do not sell personal information.

  • Apple. Sign-in, App Store purchases, and related account features.
  • Google. Optional Sign in with Google.

Community book ratings, if you participate, are stored as an anonymous or account-linked vote on a book — not as a child’s profile.

6. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

In Short: Optional Pro features may use AI. They are not required to keep a journal.

When you use a Pro librarian feature, we send the minimum book and journal facts needed for that job to an AI provider (including OpenAI) through our backend. Do not include information in notes that you would not want processed for that feature.

How to opt out. Do not use Pro librarian features, or cancel your subscription in Apple subscriptions. You can also delete journal notes or your account in Settings → Privacy & Data.
