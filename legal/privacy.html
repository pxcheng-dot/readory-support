Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

Readory is a children’s reading journal for families. A parent or guardian manages the account. We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising to children.

What we collect

  • Account (email, or sign-in with Apple or Google)
  • Child profiles you add (first name, age or birth year, optional photo)
  • Reading journal (titles, authors, covers, dates, notes, time)
  • Optional location, only if you turn it on, for nearby libraries and the right stores
  • Apple purchase and subscription status (not your full payment details)
  • Basic diagnostics to keep the app working

How we use it

  • To keep your journal, Discover, and Living Library on this device — and across devices when you sign in
  • To suggest books from what you have already logged
  • If you subscribe to Readory Pro, to run librarian features through our backend. API keys never live in the app
  • To restore purchases

We do not use children’s reading data to advertise to them.

Children

The signed-in adult is the account holder. Child profiles exist so the journal can be age-right. A child does not need their own account.

Third parties

  • Apple — sign-in, purchases
  • Google — optional sign-in
  • Supabase — account and synced journal
  • An AI provider, only when you use a Pro librarian feature, with the book facts needed for that request

Book metadata may come from public catalogs. Those lookups use a book identifier or title — not your child’s name.

Export and delete

Settings → Privacy & Data. Deleting the app does not delete a signed-in cloud account. Use Delete everything, or write to us.

Location

Optional. A one-time check can show nearby libraries and the right stores for your country. We do not use location to track children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.
